A Lean 4 solution to the `THCFactorization` spec in google-deepmind/formal-quantum-computation (`QuantumComputationSpecs/Primitives/THCFactorization.lean`): all seven holes filled, `thc_correctness` and `thc_t_cost` proved as stated. The Lean comparator accepted it in its bubblewrap sandbox (stock config plus `--challenge-from-export`), with only the axioms propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice.

The block encoding is exact for every N and M and uses no Toffoli gates. The current version (P4.10, 2026-11-26) proves 42,684 T per walk step at the Reiher FeMoco parameters (N = 54, M = 350) and 66,728 T at Li's (N = 76, M = 450). That is 0.969x and 0.984x Lee et al.'s per-step cost (derived from their Table III, which carries about ±1%): 3.1% below at Reiher, outside that uncertainty, and 1.6% below at Li, only just outside it. Lee et al.'s per-step count includes about 32 Toffolis that the spec does not charge, which narrows the margins to 2.8% and 1.4%. Over a whole phase estimation the proved total is 2.19e10 and 1.63e11 T, which is 1.03x and 1.27x Lee et al.'s totals, with 9,287 and 14,753 logical qubits against their 2,142 and 2,196. The totals stay above Lee et al.'s because of alias padding that the spec's exact block encoding forces. This is a per-step result, not an improvement in total cost or qubits, and not a FeMoco runtime estimate.

Two earlier versions are kept in the entry: P4.9 (43,868 and 67,944 T per step, 0.996x and 1.002x) and the first version, P4.4 (57,708 and 95,308 T, 1.31x and 1.41x).

Status: Settled, unreviewed (the Lean statement; the comparator checked each solution against the spec and nothing more). Google has not reviewed it.
