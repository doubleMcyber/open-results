Erdős problem 639, small cases. In a 2-colouring of the edges of K_n, at most 10, 12 and 16 edges lie in no monochromatic triangle for n = 6, 7, 8 (known, Keevash and Sudakov 2004). New here: the colourings attaining these maxima number exactly 360, 5,950 and 14,630, forming 1, 5 and 5 classes up to vertex relabelling and colour swap. For n = 8 the uncovered edges of every extremal colouring form K₄,₄.

The proof is an exhaustive computation, reproduced by two separately written enumerations. No claim is made for n ≥ 9.
